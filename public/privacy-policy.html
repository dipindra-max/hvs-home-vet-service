Privacy Policy

Home Vet Service (HVS) respects the privacy of visitors and customers. This page explains the general types of information that may be processed when you use this website.

Information you provide

If you submit a booking or review, the website may collect information such as your name, phone number, pet type, requested service, appointment details, address, optional location coordinates and review information. This information is used to process requests, communicate about appointments and manage reviews.

Location information

GPS location is optional. The booking page requests location only when you choose to use the location feature. You can provide an address or landmark instead.

Cookies and advertising

This website may use Google AdSense and related Google advertising technology. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies as described in Google's policies. Advertising availability is subject to Google's review and policies.

Third-party services

The website may link to services such as Google Maps and Facebook. Their own privacy policies apply when you use those services.

Contact

For questions about information submitted through the HVS website, contact HVS directly using the contact details shown on the home page.
