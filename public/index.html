About HVS

Local service for pet owners in Kathmandu Valley

Home Vet Service (HVS) is a local home-visit service associated with Jitendra Yadav, Veterinary Technician.

The goal of HVS is simple: make routine pet-care appointments more convenient for owners who may prefer support at home. HVS currently serves Kathmandu, Bhaktapur and Lalitpur.

Appointments are arranged directly. When a home visit is requested, customers can provide their address, landmark and optional GPS location. This helps reduce confusion about the visit location.

Frequently asked questions

Common HVS questions

Which areas does HVS cover?

HVS currently lists Kathmandu, Bhaktapur and Lalitpur as its service areas. Always confirm availability before travelling or arranging a visit.

Can I request a home visit for a dog or cat?

Yes. HVS is designed for home-visit pet-care support, primarily for dogs and cats. The suitability of a home visit depends on the situation.

Can I share my exact location?

Yes. The existing booking system can request GPS coordinates after you choose to use your current location. You may also provide an address or landmark.

Does a home visit replace emergency veterinary care?

No. If your pet appears seriously ill, injured or in immediate danger, seek prompt care from a qualified veterinary professional or veterinary clinic.

How do I confirm an appointment?

Submit your request or call HVS. The service provider should confirm the appointment before you rely on the visit time.
