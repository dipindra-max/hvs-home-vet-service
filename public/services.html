HVS SERVICES

Home Vet Service pet-care services

HVS provides home-visit support for suitable pet-care requests in Kathmandu, Bhaktapur and Lalitpur. Appointment availability should always be confirmed before a visit.

Vaccination support

HVS can help arrange convenient home vaccination support for scheduled pet-care needs when appropriate. Vaccination schedules and products should be determined with proper veterinary guidance.

General pet-care support

Home assessment and basic care support may be suitable for some routine concerns. More serious conditions may need a veterinary clinic.

Dog care

Support for dog owners who need convenient home appointments or guidance about arranging appropriate pet-care services.

Cat care

Home support for cats when the situation is suitable for a home appointment and the animal can be safely handled at home.

Home visits

Customers can provide an address, landmark and optional GPS location to make the appointment location easier to find.

Appointment guidance

Contact HVS before the visit so the service provider can understand the request and confirm whether a home visit is suitable.

When a clinic may be more appropriate

Some situations need equipment, imaging, laboratory testing, surgery or continuous monitoring that cannot be provided during a routine home visit. In such cases, seek prompt care from a qualified veterinary professional or veterinary clinic.
