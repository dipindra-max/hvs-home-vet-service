HOME VET SERVICE

About Home Vet Service

Home Vet Service (HVS) is a local home-visit pet-care service associated with Jitendra Yadav, Veterinary Technician. HVS serves pet owners in Kathmandu, Bhaktapur and Lalitpur.

Our approach

HVS focuses on convenience: owners can contact the service, describe their pet's needs, share their address or landmark, and arrange an appointment when a home visit is appropriate.

The service is intended for suitable home-care situations. Serious or emergency cases may require prompt evaluation at a veterinary clinic or by another qualified veterinary professional.

Service areas

HVS currently lists Kathmandu, Bhaktapur and Lalitpur as its service areas. Availability can vary by location and appointment time, so customers should confirm before arranging a visit.

Who is this website for?

This website is for pet owners who want information about HVS, home-visit arrangements and general pet-care guidance. It also provides contact details for appointment requests.
